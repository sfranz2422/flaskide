{# The editor's New tab: the student's app, running in a tab of its own. It is the editor's preview and nothing else — the same runtime (flask.js), the same little browser (preview.js), the same sandboxed frame — with its own Python. The app has to run HERE: every page it serves comes from Python, and the editor's Python is in another tab this one cannot reach. So this tab boots Pyodide and Flask itself and runs its own copy of the app, from the files the editor handed over through this browser's storage (see runInNewTab in app.js). Nothing is on the server. Its own copy means its own state: a list the app keeps in memory, or a data.db it writes, starts fresh here and is separate from the editor's. #}
