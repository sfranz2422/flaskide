Questions in your notes

A question is a fenced block that starts with ```quiz and ends with ```, anywhere in an assignment's or a lesson's notes. Students answer each one once. Choices and short answers are marked straight away; a long response waits for you. The points go on top of your own score for the work, and Sync sends the total to Classroom.

points: sets what it's worth, and is 1 if you leave it out. Students never see the answers: they're taken out before the notes leave the server.
